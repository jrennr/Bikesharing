# NYC bike sharing exploration

A historical exploration of August 2019 Citi Bike trip patterns and visualizations, prepared as an input to a proposed Des Moines service.

![Average trip duration](Average%20Trip%20Duration.png)

The NYC patterns suggest questions about commute hours, station placement, and rider mix. They cannot by themselves establish demand or financial viability in Des Moines; local demand, weather, density, and costs would need separate validation.
